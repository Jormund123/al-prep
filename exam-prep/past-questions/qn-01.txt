point).
18 tasks:
• tasks 1-2 gave 10 points
• tasks 3-18 gave 5 points (for these I don’t recall the exact order, but this
shouldnt matter, as they were all independent from eachother)
We were given paper to write on, the 10pt tasks had 1.5-2pages of free space
for our solutions.

notes), that were not part of the task-descriptions when necessary.

Task 2 Evolutionary Algorithm [10pt]
Define all parts and steps of an Evolutionary Algorithm. What are the benefits
and what are downsides? Use this to define an EA that solves the following
scenario:
You want to find the best tasting soda. The ingredients are (here 6 ingredients
like water, carbon dioxide or sugar were named. The exact names don’t matter).
Then you can also add up to Y=15 additives out of possible Z=60. Every
additive mustn’t be more than 1% of the mixture and all additives together
mustn’t be more than 3% of the mixture.
You may also use a council of students that rate the sodas. The best comparison
is achieved, when two sodas are compared.
