\section*{Assignment 25}
\begin{problem}
Describe the system called von Neumann’s Universal Constructor.
\end{problem}
\begin{answer}
John Von Neumann's universal constructor is a theoretical self-replicating machine which uses a 2-D cellular automata and is capabale of both computation and automaton.\\\\
The requirements are:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Several computational elements
    \item A manipulating element
    \item Cutting, Fusing and Sensing elements
    \item Girders that build the chassis and the information carrier
\end{itemize}

The characteristics are:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Lives on a virtually infinite rectangular grid with an unlimited supply of elements
    \item Has 29 different states for the elements (cells)
    \item Has a construction unit
    \item Has a construction arm (hand, cutting, fusing, sensing capabilities)
    \item Has a tape unit
    \item Has an infinite tape
    \item Consists of approximately 150{,}000 elements
\end{itemize}
So, the machine with 29 different states could process signals and perform logical operations. It used a tape containing instructions and a construction arm to build an exact copy of itself including the instruction tape.

\end{answer}

\section*{Assignment 26}
\begin{problem}
Explain how Chou-Reggia’s Loop is reproducing itself. Depict the development of the first 3 steps of Chou-Reggia’s Loop. 
\end{problem}
\begin{answer}
It reproduces by growing a guided “construction arm” from the parent loop, which traces and builds a new closed loop. Once the structure is complete, the daughter loop detaches and becomes an independent copy.
\begin{itemize}
    \item Step 0: Stable parent loop exists
    \item Step 1: Construction arm activates and starts extending outward
    \item Step 2: Arm continues growth and begins shaping the outline of a new loop
\end{itemize}
\end{answer}

\section*{Assignment 27}
\begin{problem}
Seek for an application of Lindenmayer Systems (even one from the lecture), describe it in your own words and address the following topics:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item What is the application trying to do? 
    \item How is the Lindenmayer System applied to do this?
    \item What are the major results within this application?
\end{enumerate}
\end{problem}
\begin{answer}
Application of Lindenmayer Systems: Plant Growth Modeling
    \begin{enumerate}
    \item It is used to realistically simulate the growth of plants such as trees, leaves, and roots using simple rule-based systems instead of manually designing complex structures.

    \item A Lindenmayer System (L-system) starts from an initial symbol (axiom) and applies rewriting rules in parallel over multiple iterations. The resulting strings are then interpreted geometrically (for example using turtle graphics), where symbols correspond to actions like moving forward or creating branches.

    \item It enables the generation of complex and realistic plant-like structures from simple rules, allows efficient procedural modeling of vegetation, and is widely used in computer graphics, animation, and procedural environment generation.
\end{enumerate}
\end{answer}

\section*{Assignment 32}
\begin{problem}
A really nice CA application is the Wahle-Schreckenberg model. What is the application trying to do? How is the CA used to do this? What are the major result within this application?
\end{problem}
\begin{answer}
\begin{itemize}
    \item It aims to simulate and understand real highway traffic, especially how traffic jams form and propagate even without obvious obstacles.
    \item The road is modeled as a 1D cellular automaton where each cell can be empty or contain a car with a discrete speed. At each time step, simple local rules update all cars in parallel: they accelerate if possible, slow down to avoid collisions, and include random slowing to model human behavior.
    \item The model reproduces realistic traffic phenomena such as spontaneous traffic jams, stop-and-go waves, and phase transitions between free flow and congested traffic, showing that complex traffic patterns can emerge from very simple rules.
\end{itemize}
\end{answer}
