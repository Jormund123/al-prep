\section*{Assignment 1}
\begin{problem}
What is the major difference between Weak Artificial Life and Strong Artificial Life?
\end{problem}
In Strong Artificial Life it is about really creating life (living machines), but in Weak Artificial Life it is mostly about simulating conditions and resulting behavior.

\section*{Assignment 2}
\begin{problem}
Name and describe a definition of live or living that has not been named during the lecture. Please cite the publication where you have found the definition in a scientific way.
\end{problem}
Plato definion; he divided life into three parts: vegetable life, animal life, and rational life. All living creatures possessed the first in the form of nutrition and reproduction, animals were additionally capable of sensation and locomotion, and humans also had rational souls.

From: Mariscal, Carlos, "Life", The Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy (Spring 2026 Edition), Edward N. Zalta \& Uri Nodelman (eds.), https://plato.stanford.edu/archives/spr2026/entries/life/.

\section*{Assignment 3}
\begin{problem}
Give the four to six criteria that you find to be most feasable to distinguish living from non living. Explain your decision.
\end{problem}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item Growth
    \item Metabolism
    \item Ontogenetic development
    \item Reproduction
    \item creation and death (decay)
\end{enumerate}
For me, intuitively, living needs growth in size and behaviorism (some kind of learning in interaction with the environment). A necessity for physical growing is metabolism. It develops/learns over time (ontogenetic) individually. Finally, it needs to reproduce itself somehow, so that there is a creation/birth-day and also an "end" of life. It is a cycle for each individual.

\section*{Assignment 8}
\begin{problem}
Langton’s Ant is sometimes called a two-dimensional Turing machine. Can you imagine why?, Try to refer to the formal definition of a Turing machine.
\end{problem}

A Turing machine is a formal mathematical model of computation consisting of an infinite tape, a tape head, a state register, and a transition function.

Langton’s Ant acts on a 2-dim inf board, is able to scan (read), turn(decide direction), flip (write) and move (move tape head) upon this "2-dim tape". And it has a transition function.
